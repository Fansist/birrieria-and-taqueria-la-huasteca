Pacific Beach's first dedicated birrieria, open since December 2021.

The recipes are the family's own, from San Luis Potosí, Mexico. Tortillas are pressed by hand on site every day. There is a breakfast menu with chilaquiles and menudo, and the kitchen makes chicken mole too.

Eat at the tables out front, take it to go, or order delivery through DoorDash, Grubhub or Uber Eats.
